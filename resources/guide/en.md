# CIDASH guide

CIDASH is the communication team's workspace: what is happening, what needs doing and what the news and social networks say about the university, all in one place. Each team works in its own space and only sees its own work. The screenshots use demo data (in Portuguese).

## Getting started

Accounts are by invitation: open the invitation link, set your password and sign in with your institutional email. Two-factor authentication is optional (Settings → Security). The sidebar lists every section; if you belong to several teams, click the team name at the top to switch. In the top bar: **Search** (`Ctrl+K`), **screen colours** (light, dark, colour or system) and the **bell** with your notifications. A click on a record in relations, campaigns and calendars opens a side preview; `Ctrl`+click opens its page.

![Search](/images/guide/pesquisa.jpg)

## Home

Answers "what needs my attention today?": open alerts, counters, today and the next days, your tasks, press by deadline, content in progress, approvals, overdue tasks per person (managers), latest news, new mentions, notices and reminders.

![Home](/images/guide/inicio.jpg)

## Briefing

A summary of the day at 07:00 on working days, and a weekly one on Mondays. **Update** refreshes today's briefing; the archive keeps past briefings as they were.

## Calendar

**New event**, or click a day. Drag an event to change its date, filter by type, person responsible or campaign, and **Subscribe** to see it in Outlook, Google Calendar or your phone.

![Calendar](/images/guide/calendario.jpg)

## Tasks

**New task**: title, people responsible and co-responsible, start date (today unless you change it; the creation date is kept and shown), deadline with a time, and priority; they are notified. Switch between Mine and Team, List and Board.

![Tasks](/images/guide/tarefas.jpg)

## Content

The editorial pipeline from Idea to Published. Only editors and managers approve (Review → Approved). Board, list and editorial calendar views.

![Content](/images/guide/conteudos.jpg)

## Campaigns

The campaign page shows its details (click **Edit** to change them), the events and content that belong to it, a campaign calendar with its important dates and deadlines (**Add important date**), and the campaign's tasks with their progress. A task can create its content in the campaign at the same time.

![Campaign](/images/guide/campanha.jpg)

## Press

Journalists' requests with deadline, people responsible and status, sorted by deadline with a traffic light.

## People of interest

Experts to suggest to the media, with a full profile (bio, career, topics, CV, photos) and **Copy to send**. **Dead or Alive** keeps the obituaries prepared in advance and the people who have died.

![Profile](/images/guide/perfil.jpg)

## Assets

The team's gallery of images, videos and graphics: upload or drop files, then add a caption, credit, category and tags. Search by free text, tag, category and type.

![Assets](/images/guide/assets.jpg)

## News and mentions

**News** groups the team's sources by story, with a 0–10 relevance score. **Mentions** are the news that match the team's monitoring rules and the social posts with its hashtags; filter by source and by network.

![Mentions](/images/guide/mencoes.jpg)

## Alerts

Event without an owner, press deadline near, content stuck in review, campaign without content, news from a priority source, source failing and spike in mentions. Alerts close by themselves when the problem is fixed.

## On any record

Comments, attachments, relations, personal reminders and history. Settings → Notifications chooses what you also receive by email.

## For managers

Settings: **Team** (invite and roles), **Sources**, **Monitoring** (rules for the news and hashtags for social networks), **Alerts**, **Types** and **Terms**. The super admin has **Administration**: teams, users, sources, social networks and email.

![Monitoring](/images/guide/monitorizacao.jpg)
