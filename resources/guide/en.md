# CIDASH guide

CIDASH is the communication team's workspace: what is happening, what needs doing and what the press says about the university, all in one place. Each team (the Rectorate's office, each faculty's communication) works in its own space and only sees its own work.

## Every day

**Home.** Answers "what needs my attention today?": open alerts, today's and upcoming events, your tasks, press requests by deadline, content in progress, notices, reminders, the latest news and the mentions to triage.

**Briefing.** At 07:00 on working days CIDASH prepares a summary of the day: agenda, deadlines, alerts and what the news said since the previous briefing. On Mondays there is also a weekly briefing. Briefings are archived as they were on the day.

**Search.** `Ctrl+K` (or `⌘K`) searches everything, without accents or whole words.

## Modules

- **Calendar:** the team's events by type, with filters. Drag an event to change its date. A click opens the preview; `Ctrl`+click opens the page. "Subscribe" gives a link for Outlook, Google Calendar or your phone.
- **Tasks:** yours and the team's, as a list or a board. A task can be created from any record and stays linked to it.
- **Content:** the editorial pipeline, from Idea to Published. Only editors and managers approve.
- **Campaigns:** objectives, audiences, dates and channels, with the events and content that belong to each campaign.
- **Press:** journalists' requests with deadline, owner and status.
- **People:** people of interest for the press, with expertise areas, bios and contacts.
- **Notices:** internal notices with a start and an end; managers can pin them.

## Monitoring

- **News:** what the team's sources published, grouped by story. Mark stories relevant or not; the 0–10 score helps choose what to read first.
- **Mentions:** news matching the team's monitoring rules. Each rule can also search Google News.
- **Alerts:** event without an owner, press deadline near, content stuck in review, campaign without content, news from a priority source, spike in mentions. Alerts close by themselves when the problem is fixed.

## On any record

Detail pages have **comments**, **attachments**, **relations** to other records, personal **reminders** and the **history** of changes.

## Notifications

The bell shows your notifications. In Settings → Notifications choose what you also receive by email, including the briefings.

## For managers

Managers set the team up in **Settings**: Team, Sources, Monitoring, Alerts, Types and Terms.

## First steps for a new team

1. The super admin creates the team and names its managers.
2. Managers invite the team.
3. They choose the news sources and the priority ones.
4. They create the monitoring rules.
5. They add the people of interest and their areas of expertise.
6. They adjust event types and content formats if needed.

The Home shows this list to managers until it is done.
